# v1.27.23 — Boss Adaptive Counterplay (isolated QA)

Marvin and the Hydra now choose melee attacks using the player's actual altitude, boost, and orbital approach **at the beginning of the windup**, not random target updates.

- High flight and active boost invite targeted tentacle grabs.
- Marvin claws punish orbiting and the slow, heavy robot form; frontal low-altitude combat invites his bite.
- In final phase, Marvin occasionally varies a frontal approach with a tentacle interception.
- Every fourth attack cycle preserves a readable ranged counterplay window.
- Target coordinates remain latched through warning and strike; moving outside the marked hit zone negates damage.
- Deterministic tests cover the reactive decisions and physical dodge zones.
